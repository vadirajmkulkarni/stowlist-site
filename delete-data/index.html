A clean shelf

Delete your data.

You control your library. Here is how to remove local data and an optional cloud copy.

Updated October 17, 2026

Start on the original device

If you have used cloud backup, use the app’s data controls while you still have the original anonymous device identity. Uninstalling the app alone does not request cloud deletion. Make an export first if you want to keep a copy.

1. Delete the optional cloud copy

  1. Open Stowlist on the device used for backup and open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete cloud copy and guest account and review its confirmation.
  3. Confirm deletion, keep an internet connection and wait for the app’s success message. This removes the Stowlist cloud document and its anonymous app identity, while keeping your local library and checklists.
  4. If a cloud action fails, the cloud copy may still exist. Read the error, try again when connected, or contact support before uninstalling.

If you have never used a cloud action, there may be no Stowlist cloud copy or cloud identity to delete.

2. Erase all Stowlist app data

To remove both the cloud copy and local app data, choose Delete all Stowlist data in Settings and review its confirmation. The app deletes the accessible cloud copy and guest account first, then erases the local item library, stored photo copies, checklist data and preferences. If cloud deletion fails, local data is kept so you can retry. Wait for the success result before uninstalling.

This does not erase original images from your photo library.

3. Remove other copies you chose to make

Delete portable export files from Files, iCloud Drive, email attachments or other destinations where you saved or shared them. Manage operating-system backups through your Apple account and device settings. Stowlist’s controls cannot erase those copies or data held by another app.

What about purchase records?

If purchasing becomes available, Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction and related records for their service, accounting, legal and security needs. Deleting Stowlist data does not erase or refund an Apple purchase. Request a refund through Apple when applicable.

If you no longer have the device

Email tinyquotesapp@gmail.com with “Stowlist data deletion” in the subject. Explain the request without sending private item contents or passwords. We may need enough information to verify and locate the record. Because cloud backup uses an anonymous app-specific identity rather than an email account, an email address alone may not identify it. We cannot promise recovery or identification after the identity is lost.

Service providers can retain limited copies for their documented security, backup or legal requirements and follow their own deletion lifecycle. The privacy policy explains retention and your choices.
